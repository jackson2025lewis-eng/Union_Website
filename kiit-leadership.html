KIIT Chapter

History of KIIT Chapter

The KIIT Chapter of the Liberian Students Union in Odisha is rooted in a strong commitment to unity, academic excellence, and student support. Since its formation, the chapter has grown into a vibrant community where Liberian students collaborate, encourage one another, and participate in cultural, intellectual, and leadership-building activities. Through peer mentorship, student engagement, and shared responsibility, the chapter continues to strengthen the sense of belonging and create opportunities for personal, academic, and professional growth.

Meet the dedicated students leading KIIT Chapter

Angeline G. Jurry

President

PhD in Biotechnology

Ms. Angeline G. Jurry is a dedicated academic leader, researcher, and student advocate committed to education, leadership, and community development. Her leadership journey began through student governance and media roles, where she developed a strong foundation in communication, mentorship, and service to the student community. She has held several significant leadership positions, including representative roles within her academic community and executive responsibilities that emphasized unity, academic excellence, and student welfare. As President of the KIIT Chapter, she continues to inspire students through inclusive leadership, strategic coordination, and a steadfast commitment to academic and personal growth. She also serves as Superintendent of Odisha State for All Liberian Students in India (ALSI), where she plays a vital role in supporting Liberian students and strengthening the student network across India.

Ms. Sandoral K. Nyandibo

Vice President

PhD in Psychology

Ms. Sandoral K. Nyandibo is a dedicated Liberian scholar, emerging psychologist, and student leader committed to academic excellence, student welfare, and community development. Currently pursuing her PhD in Psychology at KIIT University, Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India, she brings a strong dedication to leadership, mentorship, and advocacy for student well-being. She holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology and Chemistry from the African Methodist Episcopal University (AME University), Liberia, and a Master of Arts degree in Psychology with a specialization in Clinical Psychology from KIIT University, India. Her academic interests focus on mental health, psychological well-being, and research that promotes positive social impact. As Vice President, she plays an active role in fostering unity, strengthening student representation, and supporting initiatives that contribute to the growth and welfare of Liberian students at KIIT and beyond.

Theresa Faith Luciny

Secretary General

Bachelor of Business Administration

Theresa Faith Luciny is a motivated and detail-oriented student leader currently pursuing her final year in BBA Finance at KIIT University, Odisha. She serves as the General Secretary of Liberian Students at KIIT and as Assistant Secretary of Liberian Students in Odisha, where she plays an active role in student coordination, communication, and event organization. Her leadership is marked by strong organizational skills, dedication to service, and a commitment to fostering unity and support among fellow students. Through her role, she continues to contribute to a vibrant and well-connected student community while promoting collaboration, accountability, and academic engagement.
